Wedding Story

Aaron & Jennifer

Project Type
Laos & American Wedding
When
August 2026
Location
Missouri City, TX

Two ceremonies, one day — a traditional Laos morning at home, and an American celebration that afternoon.

Aaron and Jennifer’s wedding was really two weddings hours apart, and photographing it meant moving between two different worlds in a single afternoon.

The morning belonged to tradition: the groom’s procession to the bride’s house, the barrier held across the doorway, the washing of his feet, the lit candle, and the blessing bracelets tied on one at a time — each a wish spoken out loud. By mid-afternoon everything had changed, and the day settled into a reception in American attire among people who had known them both a long time.

A day like this rewards preparation. The Laos ceremony moves fast and does not pause for a photographer, so every moment had to be anticipated rather than reacted to.

★★★★★

“Jag exceeded our expectations in every way! Aside from his work being outstanding, he was extremely easy to work with and very professional throughout our small but somewhat unique (traditional Laos style and American style) morning/afternoon wedding. He went above and beyond every step of the way, and his work is worth every penny of the investment!”
