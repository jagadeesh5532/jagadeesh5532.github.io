Better Baseball VIP Red Carpet Event

Project Type
VIP Red Carpet Event · Two Days
When
Fall 2026
Location
2717 W. Grand Pkwy N., Park Row, Katy, TX
Client
Better Baseball

Two days, a red carpet, and a baseball superstore’s first step into Texas.

Better Baseball is the Georgia retailer that calls itself “America’s Baseball Superstore.” Its flagship is in Marietta, near Atlanta, and Katy is its first Texas location. Before the doors opened to the public, the company held a VIP red carpet event and invited influencers from across the country to see it first.

They chose Joyful & Graceful Moments to photograph it. The event ran over two days, so the job was to cover the whole thing: guests arriving along the red carpet, the introductions and conversations, the gear and the store itself, and the small moments in between that a company can use afterwards.

This was one of the most demanding shoots I take on, because the photographs were part of the experience. VIP guests were photographed on the red carpet, and those portraits had to be edited, printed and framed within minutes, so each guest could take their framed photo home before leaving the store. That meant shooting, culling, retouching and printing at the same time, with no room to fall behind.

To make that work I brought in a second photographer, as I do for critical events. One of us covered the carpet and the VIP portraits while the other covered the store, the guests and the candid moments, so nothing was missed and the editing could keep moving.

For an event that exists to be shared, the photographs matter as much as the evening. Influencers post in real time, so the images had to keep up with them and still look consistent and clean once the weekend was over.

Why Katy, and why Park Row?

Better Baseball is moving into 2717 W. Grand Pkwy. N. in the Park Row development on the Grand Parkway, in Katy, west of Houston. As reported by Community Impact, the Katy-Fulshear Times and Hoodline, it will be the company’s first store in Texas and only its second location.

The area makes sense for a baseball store. Katy and the Grand Parkway corridor are among the busiest youth sports areas in the Houston region, with school, club and travel teams all within a short drive. The store carries gloves, bats, catcher’s gear, hats and cleats from brands including Wilson, Rawlings, Louisville Slugger, Mizuno, adidas and New Balance. The company’s Georgia flagship also has a bat fitting lab with batting cages, and the store’s general manager of operations, Matthew Marks, has described the Katy store as “a world class immersive experience.”

If you are opening a store, restaurant or office anywhere in Houston, Katy, Cypress, Sugar Land or The Woodlands and want the launch covered properly, you can see how corporate event photography works, read a little about me, or get in touch with your dates. For multi-day events and VIP evenings, it helps to talk through the schedule and the shots the brand wants a week or two ahead.

Questions about VIP & brand events

Who can photograph a VIP red carpet event in Katy?

Joyful & Graceful Moments covers VIP and red carpet events in Katy, Houston and the surrounding suburbs, including store openings, brand launches and influencer events. This page shows the two-day Better Baseball event at Park Row.

Can photos be edited and printed during the event?

Yes. For this event the VIP portraits were edited, printed and framed within minutes, so guests could take a framed photo home before they left. It needs extra planning on equipment and staffing, so tell me early if you want it.

Do you bring a second photographer?

For critical or fast-paced events I bring a second photographer, so one person can cover the key portraits while the other covers the room, the guests and the candid moments.

How fast is delivery for corporate and brand events?

Selects can be delivered the same day for social media, and the full edited gallery follows within a few days. Live editing and on-site printing is available when the event calls for it.

Do you photograph multi-day events?

Yes. This event ran over two days. For multi-day coverage I agree the schedule and the must-have shots with you beforehand.
