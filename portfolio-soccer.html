Soccer Photography

Match and training photography following the action on and off the ball.

Ninety minutes, one frame

Soccer is unforgiving to photograph. The ball moves faster than you can pan, the action happens at the far end just as you commit to the near one, and the moment worth keeping lasts a fraction of a second.

I shoot youth and club matches around Houston — league games, tournaments, and team and individual portraits at the end of a season. I work the sideline and follow the play rather than standing in one spot, which means more frames with your child actually in them.

Parents usually come away with something they could never get from the stands: their own player, sharp, mid-stride, face visible, in the middle of doing the thing they have been practising all year.
