A photograph that still looks like you

Most people dislike their own headshot because it does not look like them — too stiff, too lit, too obviously taken in a studio against a grey wall.

I shoot headshots on location around Houston and Spring: outside at CityPlace, in a park, somewhere with daylight and a background that is not a backdrop. Therapists, actors, business owners, people who need something for a website or a profile and want to look approachable rather than corporate.

Sessions are short. Bring two or three tops, allow an hour, and expect to be talked at a fair bit — most of the good frames happen in the gaps between poses, when you have stopped bracing for the shutter.
