Corporate & Events

Conferences, open houses, award nights and non-profit events, delivered fast enough to still be useful.

Your organisation, on the day it mattered

Corporate events are photographed for a reason. Marketing needs images, leadership needs coverage, and somebody has to document the thing the organisation spent six months planning.

I have photographed the Society of Petroleum Engineers Artificial Lift Conference at the Marriott in The Woodlands and a bank’s open house with clients, staff and local officials in the same room. Both needed the same things: keynote and panel coverage, candid networking, clean portraits of leadership, and a usable set of images delivered quickly enough to still matter.

I also work with non-profit organisations, where the brief is usually different — donors, volunteers and the people the work is actually for, photographed in a way the organisation can use for a year without it feeling staged.

What you get

  • Keynote, panel and stage coverage
  • Candid networking and floor photography
  • Clean leadership and team portraits on the day
  • Web-ready and print-resolution files, sized for press and social
  • Fast turnaround — often a couple of days, sometimes under twenty-four hours
  • Non-profit and community organisations welcome

Questions people ask

How fast can you deliver conference photos?

Faster than anything else I do, because corporate images lose value by the week. A couple of days is normal and under twenty-four hours is often possible. If you need a selection on the night for social, say so and I will cut a set during the event.

Can you photograph a multi-day conference?

Yes. Tell me the agenda and which sessions matter most and I will build coverage around it.

Do you take headshots at events?

Yes — setting up a corner for staff or speaker headshots during a conference is common and efficient, since everyone is already there and dressed for it.

Do you work with non-profits?

Yes. Fundraisers, volunteer days, community programmes and donor events.

Who owns the images?

You get full use of the delivered images for your own marketing, press and internal communications. I retain copyright and the right to show the work in my own portfolio — if any part of your event is sensitive and should not be shown publicly, tell me and it stays out.
