Sports Photography

Soccer matches, swim meets and end-of-season team portraits around Houston.

Shot from the sideline

Sports photography is mostly anticipation. By the time you see the goal it is already gone, so the work is in reading the play and being pointed at the right part of the field a second early.

I photograph soccer matches and swim meets around Houston and the surrounding suburbs — league games, tournaments, meets, and team and individual portraits at the end of a season. On a pitch I work the sideline and follow the play rather than standing in one spot; on a pool deck I work from fixed positions with the heat sheet, because deck space is tight and the moments are narrow.

I am the official swim and event photographer for the Northpoint Shockwaves, and I shoot for TASC, the Tomball Aquatic Swim Club. Season-long club work is different from a one-off booking: you learn the swimmers, you know who is in which heat, and by March you are photographing children you first met in September.

Parents end up with the photograph they can never take from the stands: their own child, in focus, mid-effort, with the face showing — and, at a meet, the look up at the board afterwards.

What you get

  • Match, meet and tournament coverage
  • End-of-season team and individual portraits
  • Galleries turned around quickly, while the season still matters
  • High-resolution files suitable for printing and framing
  • Club and league bookings welcome — official photographer for Northpoint Shockwaves and TASC
  • Individual family bookings for a single player

Questions people ask

Do you photograph the whole team or just my child?

Either. Families book me for a single player, and clubs book me for a whole squad across a season. The approach differs — tell me which you need.

How quickly are match photos delivered?

Quickly, because a season moves on. Nobody wants last month’s match three weeks after it finished.

Can you do end-of-season team portraits?

Yes — team shots and individual portraits in kit, usually at the ground before or after a session.

Do you shoot indoor pools?

Yes. Pool decks are crowded and awkward, so I work from fixed positions with the heat sheet rather than moving about, which keeps me out of the officials’ way.

How far will you travel for a match?

Houston and the surrounding suburbs are routine. I also take travel assignments further afield, with travel costs added.
