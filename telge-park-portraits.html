A local Harris County park in Cypress, with trees, trails and soft light.

Telge Park sits on Pleasant Grove Road in Cypress, and it is a great place to photograph portraits close to home. It has what a portrait needs: layered trees for a soft background, shaded clearings, and a calm, wooded setting that stays green through most of the year.

This page shows a selection from a fall portrait session there, photographed in November 2026, when the trees and trails take on warm autumn tones. The park’s natural trails and open clearings give plenty of variety in a single session, so you can move between a few different looks without driving anywhere else.

If you live in Cypress, Bridgeland, Fairfield, Towne Lake, Tomball or nearby, a local park like this means a short drive, no travel time and a relaxed session. I photograph portraits, families, seniors and couples across the Cypress area.

Telge Park portraits: common questions

Do I need a permit to take portraits at Telge Park?

Rules can change, so please confirm current photography policies with Harris County Precinct 3 before your session. If you book with me, I will help you plan around the park’s guidelines.

What is the best time of day for portraits at Telge Park?

Late afternoon, about an hour or two before sunset, gives soft, warm light through the trees. Early morning also works well and is usually quieter.

When is the best time for fall photos in Cypress?

Cypress colors arrive later than up north, usually mid-November through early December. Late afternoon light makes the warm tones in the trees and trails stand out.

How do I book a portrait session in Cypress?

Send me your preferred date, group size and the kind of session you want through the contact page, and I will reply with availability and details. Sessions include an online gallery of edited photos.

Is there parking at Telge Park?

Parking is limited, so plan to arrive a few minutes early and carpool if you can. I will share arrival and meeting details when you book.

What should we wear for fall portraits?

Choose colors that complement the surroundings, such as deep greens, burgundy, rust, mustard, cream and denim, and avoid large logos. My What to Wear guide has more ideas.

Can you photograph families, couples and seniors at Telge Park?

Yes. I photograph family portraits, couples, seniors, headshots and milestone sessions at local parks across Cypress, Tomball and the Houston area.

Fall portraits in Cypress

Fall is one of the best seasons for portraits at Telge Park. Color arrives later in Cypress than up north, usually mid-November into December, and the warm tones in the trees and trails pair well with deep greens, burgundy, rust, mustard and cream. Late afternoon light makes it glow.

Who a local park session suits

Families get space for kids to move, couples get quiet corners and soft light, seniors get a natural backdrop, and expecting parents get a calm, shaded setting. Headshots and milestone portraits work well here too.

Areas near Telge Park

Telge Park is a short drive from Cypress, Bridgeland, Towne Lake, Fairfield, Copperfield, Jersey Village and Tomball. I photograph portraits across all of these areas and throughout Houston. See all the areas I serve or browse more photo locations around Houston.

Portraits at Telge Park, Cypress

Telge Park is a Harris County Precinct 3 park in Cypress, with wooded natural-surface trails, picnic areas and access toward Cypress Creek. For portraits, the trees give you soft, even shade and a layered green background, and the clearings give you room to change the look without leaving the park.

Practical notes if you are planning one: parking is limited, so arrive a little early and carpool if you can; late afternoon is usually the most flattering for light, Houston heat means a shaded spot and some water are worth bringing, and bug spray is wise near the creek. Please check with Harris County Precinct 3 for current park hours and any rules for professional photography before booking, and I will help you plan the timing.
