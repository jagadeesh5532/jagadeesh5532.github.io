Professional Headshots

On-location portraits for websites, profiles and auditions — turned around in days, sometimes hours.

A photograph that still looks like you

Most people dislike their own headshot because it does not look like them. Too stiff, too lit, too obviously taken against a grey wall in somebody’s back room.

I shoot headshots on location around Houston and Spring — outside at CityPlace, in a park, anywhere with daylight and a background that is not a backdrop. Therapists who need to look approachable rather than corporate, actors who need audition shots, business owners updating a website, people who simply need one good photograph of themselves.

Sessions are short. Bring two or three tops, allow an hour, and expect to be talked at a fair bit — most of the usable frames happen in the gaps between poses, once you have stopped bracing for the shutter.

What you get

  • Shot on location in daylight, not against a studio backdrop
  • Two to three outfit changes within the session
  • Retouching on the selects, kept natural
  • Files sized for LinkedIn, websites, casting profiles and print
  • Fast turnaround — often within a couple of days, sometimes under twenty-four hours
  • Outfit advice beforehand if you want it

Questions people ask

How quickly can I get my headshots?

Headshots are the fastest work I do. A couple of days is normal and under twenty-four hours is often possible. If you have a deadline, say so when you book.

What should I wear?

Solid colours, nothing with a busy pattern, and necklines you are comfortable in. Bring two or three options — it costs nothing and gives you a range to choose from.

Do you shoot in a studio?

I shoot on location in daylight. It reads as more natural and more approachable than a studio backdrop, which is what most people actually want from a headshot now.

How long does a session take?

About an hour, including outfit changes and moving between two or three spots.

Can you do a whole team?

Yes. Team and staff headshots at your office or somewhere nearby, shot consistently so they sit together on a website. Get in touch with the headcount and I will work out the time needed.
