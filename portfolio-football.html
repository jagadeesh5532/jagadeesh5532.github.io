Football Photography

Game-day action from The Woodlands, including Trinity Lutheran School vs. The Woodlands Christian Academy (2024).

Fast game, one frame

Football is fast and crowded. Players collide, the play moves across the field in seconds, and the moment worth keeping is often the one you nearly missed.

I shoot school and club football games around Houston and The Woodlands, from full games to sideline action and team portraits. I follow the play rather than standing in one spot, so your player is in more frames, not just the ones after the whistle.

The photos on this page are from Trinity Lutheran School vs. The Woodlands Christian Academy in 2024, shot at 5800 Academy Way, The Woodlands, TX 77384.
