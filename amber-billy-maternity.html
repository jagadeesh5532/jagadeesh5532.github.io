Maternity Story

Amber & Billy

Project Type
Maternity Session
When
May 2026
Location
El Jardin Beach, TX

Shot around sunset, for the golden light.

Amber and Billy stayed overnight close to the beach so there was no chance of missing the golden hour — they arrived unhurried and ready.

It was beautiful to work in. As the sun drops the light keeps changing, and that was exactly the look we wanted for her: her reference poses first while it was still high, then the close frames as it fell toward the water.
