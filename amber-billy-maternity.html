Maternity Story

Amber & Billy

Project Type
Maternity Session
When
May 2026
Location
El Jardin Beach, Seabrook, TX

Shot around sunset, for the golden light.

Amber and Billy stayed overnight close to the beach so there was no chance of missing the golden hour — they arrived unhurried and ready.

It was beautiful to work in. As the sun drops the light keeps changing, and that was exactly the look we wanted for her: her reference poses first while it was still high, then the close frames as it fell toward the water.

★★★★★

“Jag was wonderful! He helped with the poses and was very quick in getting the photos back to us. We loved the different lighting and edits in the photos so they don’t all look the same! Thank you Jag!!”

Amber Smith — Maternity Session

Thinking about El Jardin Beach for your maternity session?

Amber and Billy made this one easy. They are a genuinely lovely couple — warm with each other in a way you cannot direct, forever laughing at something only the two of them were in on, and completely unbothered by the camera. When a couple is that comfortable together, the photographs mostly happen on their own.

They picked well, too. El Jardin Beach in Seabrook is a small city park on Galveston Bay, about half an hour southeast of Houston, and it is one of the kindest beaches near the city for photographs. The sand is soft, the water is calm, and because the shoreline faces west you get the sun setting straight out over the bay rather than behind you — which is the whole reason to shoot a maternity session here in the first place.

It is far quieter than Galveston, so you are not working around crowds, and there is free parking, restrooms and a shower right there for an outfit change. Come about ninety minutes before sunset and you will have warm, low light the whole way through.

If you are expecting and thinking about a beach session around Houston, Seabrook, League City or Galveston, I would love to hear about it. You can see how a maternity session works, read a little about me, or get in touch about your dates. Somewhere around thirty-two to thirty-six weeks is usually the sweet spot.

El Jardin Beach
500 El Jardin Drive, Seabrook, TX
City of Seabrook park · free parking, restrooms and showers · no lifeguard on duty
