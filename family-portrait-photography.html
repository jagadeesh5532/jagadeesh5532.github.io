Family & Portraits

On-location sessions for families, milestones and anyone who needs a photograph that actually looks like them.

The faces you already know by heart

Portrait sessions are the ones families come back for. A year passes, a child grows out of a phase, somebody graduates, and the photographs on the wall are suddenly out of date.

These happen wherever makes sense for the people in them — an evening with a mother and her girls in Tomball, a family on holiday at the beach in Galveston, a father and son on the morning of a graduation in Pearland, a first birthday with cake everywhere. Reunions, Fourth of July mornings, three generations in one frame because everyone finally came.

I do not over-direct. I will tell you where to stand and then mostly leave you alone, because the frame worth keeping is almost never the one where everybody is looking at the camera. It is the one half a second later, when somebody laughs.

What you get

  • An online gallery with high-resolution files for printing and web-sized copies for sharing
  • No cap on the number of edited photographs — typically at least thirty to forty from an hour
  • Normal turnaround is five to seven days
  • Posing help throughout, and I’ll happily work from inspiration photos you like
  • Outfit and location suggestions beforehand if you want them
  • Prints and photo books available on request once the gallery is delivered
  • Pets are welcome — bring them

Questions people ask

How long is a family session?

Usually forty-five minutes to an hour and a half. With small children, shorter and earlier is almost always better than longer and later.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes. Pets are welcome and they often loosen everyone up, which helps more than it hurts. Just tell me beforehand so I can pick a location that suits.

How many photos will we get?

I don’t put a cap on it — you get everything worth keeping. For a one-hour session that is usually at least thirty to forty edited images, and often considerably more.

Will you help us pose?

Yes. Most people have no idea what to do with their hands, and that is normal. I will place you, adjust as we go, and work from inspiration photos if you have seen something you like.

Can you suggest a location?

Yes — tell me the size of the group, the time you can get there and the look you are after, and I will suggest somewhere that works. I shoot all over the Houston area.
