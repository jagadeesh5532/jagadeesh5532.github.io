Houston Senior Portrait Photographer

Natural, personality-first portraits for the student who is about to leave school behind.

A senior session that looks like them

Senior portraits are for the student, not just the yearbook. I photograph seniors across Houston, Conroe, The Woodlands and Cypress, outdoors in natural light, with very little posing and plenty of room for their personality.

Most sessions include the classic cap and gown portraits and a few outfit changes, and many include the thing they love: a drum kit, a guitar, a ball, a camera, a horse. That is what makes a senior gallery theirs. A drummer’s session started in the music room at home and finished at a nearby park in cap and gown.

Parents and siblings are welcome in the frame. Some of the pictures families end up framing are the relaxed ones with everyone laughing.

Looking for something else? See family portraits, headshots or birthday and celebration photography, or read how I cover Houston and the surrounding areas.

What you get

  • Classic cap and gown portraits and outfit changes
  • Time for the thing your senior loves: an instrument, sports gear, a hobby
  • Relaxed family photographs and candids alongside the portraits
  • Location and outfit suggestions beforehand if you want them
  • An online gallery with high-resolution files for prints and web-sized copies for sharing
  • Normal turnaround is five to seven days

Questions people ask

When should we book a senior session?

Early in the school year works well, from late summer through fall, before the calendar fills up with football, exams and applications. Spring sessions work too if you want the cap and gown shots closer to graduation.

What should my senior bring?

Their cap and gown if they have it, one or two outfits that feel like them, and anything that matters to them: an instrument, sports gear, a car, a camera. The best senior photographs show who they are, not just what they look like.

Where do you shoot senior portraits?

Parks, campuses, downtown streets, the neighbourhood around your home, or at home itself. I work around Houston, Conroe, The Woodlands and Cypress, and I will suggest a location based on what your senior likes.

Can parents and siblings be in the photos?

Yes. I usually take a few relaxed family frames and some candids alongside the portraits, and they are often the ones families print.

How long does a senior session take?

Usually an hour to an hour and a half. If we shoot at home first and then at a park, allow a little more time for the move.

What happens if it rains?

We reschedule to a day that suits you. Outdoor sessions depend on the weather, and a calm evening is worth waiting for.
