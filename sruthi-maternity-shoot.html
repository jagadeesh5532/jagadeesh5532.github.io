Maternity Story

Sruthi’s Maternity Session

Project Type
Maternity Session
When
2026
Location
Oyster Creek Park, Sugar Land, TX

A short evening session, timed for the last of the light.

Oyster Creek Park in Sugar Land, late in the day, with the whole session built around the half hour before sunset. We kept it short and unhurried — there is no reason to make anyone stand around for two hours at that stage.

The family brought a chalkboard along to mark the countdown, which I like: it dates the photographs without anybody having to explain them later.

Evening light at that park does something generous to a maternity session, and we had just enough of it.

Expecting? Let’s plan your session.
