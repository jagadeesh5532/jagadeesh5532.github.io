Maudie Byrd’s Birthday

Project Type
Birthday Celebration
When
June 2026
Location
Galveston, TX

Sisters, cousins, children and grandchildren — all of them in one room, on one evening.

Some birthdays are a party. This one was a reunion. Maudie opened her home in Galveston and her family came from everywhere — sisters who had known each other a lifetime, extended family who rarely get to stand in the same room, and the generations that came after them.

That is what made this milestone birthday worth photographing properly. Gatherings like this are rarer than anyone admits. The people in these frames have decades of history between them, and an evening where all of them are together, laughing, is not something a family can simply arrange again next year.

So I photographed it as what it was: not a party to document, but a family to remember. Every sister. Every pairing. Every quiet moment on the deck between the noise.

They loved the gallery so much they ordered photo books — which is the best thing a family can tell a photographer. These were never meant to live on a screen.

★★★★★

“Pictures took place at my home for a very special birthday party. Photographer was nice, professional and did a wonderful job catching the party spirit. I would highly recommend for any important event.”

Maudie Byrd — Birthday Party Photography
