Azlaan’s 3rd Birthday

Project Type
Birthday Celebration
When
September 2026
Location
Houston, TX

No posing, no backdrop — just three-year-olds at full speed.

Azlaan’s mum didn’t want a traditional setup. She wanted the party photographed where it was actually happening: an indoor play space, kids running loose, everyone in the middle of having fun.

That is harder than it sounds. Nothing holds still, the light is whatever the room gives you, and you cannot ask a three-year-old to do it again. So you stop directing and start anticipating — staying close, reading where the next burst of joy is about to come from, and being ready before it arrives.

Which is the whole point. Photographs of children told where to stand look like photographs of children told where to stand. These look like the afternoon actually felt: loud, fast and full of it, with the family frames and the cake cutting folded in when everyone came back to the room.
