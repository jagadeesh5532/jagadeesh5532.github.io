What I Photograph

Nine kinds of work, all of it on location, all of it around people.

Different days, the same approach

A wedding, a swim meet and a conference have almost nothing in common on paper. What they share is that the moment worth keeping lasts about a second and does not come back, and the photographer either saw it coming or did not.

So the approach is the same across all of it: find out beforehand how the day runs and what matters to the people in it, stay out of the way, and be pointed in the right direction when it happens. Everything below is shot on location — your home, a venue, a park, a pool deck — around Cypress, Houston and the surrounding suburbs — and further afield for travel assignments.

If what you need is not on this list, ask anyway. Most of what I do now started with somebody asking.
