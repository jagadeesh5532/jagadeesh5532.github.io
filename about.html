About Me

A photographer passionate about capturing the joy, energy, and authenticity of life's most important moments.

My Journey

Since 2010, I've had the privilege of capturing life's most colorful and meaningful moments. What started as a passion for photography has evolved into a mission: to preserve the joy, energy, and authenticity of every celebration.

Based in Houston, Texas, I specialize in Indian cultural events, family celebrations, and competitive sports photography. From vibrant wedding ceremonies to intense athletic moments, I bring technical skill and emotional connection to every shoot.

My approach is simple: listen to your story, understand what matters most to you, and capture those moments with authenticity and artistry. Every photograph is a bridge between the moment and the memory.

Every event is unique, every family has a different story, and every athlete has their own journey. My job is to see what makes your moment special and translate that into images you'll treasure forever.
