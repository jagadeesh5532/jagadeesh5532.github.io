The Person Behind the Lens

About Me..

I'm a Houston-based photographer with a deep passion for capturing authentic moments. What started as a personal hobby over 15 years ago has grown into a meaningful practice of visual storytelling, driven by my unwavering passion to preserve the beauty in everyday life.

I specialize in family portraits, children's photography, sports, celebrations, and travel photography. Whether it's the intensity of athletic competition, the vibrant colors of a cultural celebration, or the quiet warmth of a family portrait — I approach every frame with curiosity, care, and creativity. My passion lies in finding the extraordinary within the ordinary, in those fleeting moments that deserve to be frozen in time.

Based in Houston but available for travel assignments, I believe every moment deserves to be preserved with honesty and beauty. It's this passion for storytelling that drives me to connect with my subjects, understand their vision, and deliver images that transcend the ordinary. My goal is simple: to give you images that move you for years to come—photographs that become a cherished part of your story.
