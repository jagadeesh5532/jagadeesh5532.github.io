Diwali Photography

Festival of lights celebrations — the colour, the lamps and the people, captured as the evening unfolds.

Diwali Event 2025

A vibrant celebration of light and tradition

Lamps, colour, and everyone dressed up

Diwali in Houston is a season rather than a single night. Community gatherings, temple celebrations, office events and family evenings at home all land within a few weeks of each other, and every one of them looks completely different.

I photograph Diwali and other Indian celebrations across the Houston area — the diyas and rangoli, the clothes people only wear once a year, the generations of a family in one frame because everyone finally came.

Having grown up with these festivals, I know roughly when the moment is coming: the aarti, the first lamp, the children with sparklers. That timing is most of it. The rest is staying out of the way and letting the evening happen.
