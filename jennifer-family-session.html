Portrait Session

Jennifer & Family

Project Type
Family Portraits
When
August 2026
Location
Tomball, TX

A mother, her girls, and one easy evening in Tomball.

Not a formal sitting — an evening together that happened to be photographed. The girls were themselves, which is the whole point, and the frames worth keeping came in the gaps rather than in the arrangements.

Families often arrive braced for an hour of being arranged. The ones that go well are the ones where everybody forgets the camera is there, and what you take home is how they actually are with each other.
