Engagement Story

Michele & Dan

Project Type
Engagement Portraits
When
September 2026
Location
Pearland, TX

Two looks, one hour, and the last of the evening light on a quiet trail outside Houston.

Michele and Dan’s brief was refreshingly specific: two portraits — one formal, one casual — to display at their wedding venue. Not a gallery to scroll through, but two images that would hang at size, in a room, in front of guests.

That changes how you shoot. A frame printed large and read from across a room has to hold up on its own, so both looks needed clean separation from the background, faces lit properly rather than incidentally, and enough room around the couple to crop to whatever the venue required.

Shadow Creek Trail gave us both settings within a short walk, and a seven o’clock start put us in low, warm light instead of overhead glare. They arrived in the formal look; the casual one followed once the sun had dropped.

★★★★★

“I can’t say enough wonderful things about my experience with this photographer! From start to finish, he was professional, patient and truly made us feel comfortable. The photos turned out absolutely beautiful. I would highly recommend Joyful and Graceful moments.”

Michele Provenzano — Pearland, TX
