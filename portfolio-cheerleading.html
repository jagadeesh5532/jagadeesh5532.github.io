Cheerleading Photography

Game-day spirit from The Woodlands: the Trinity Lutheran School cheer squad at the football game against The Woodlands Christian Academy.

Spirit on the sideline

Cheer is fast and loud, and a lot of it happens in the seconds before a play: the stunt going up, the jump at the top, the squad turning to face the crowd. Those are easy to miss if you only watch the field.

I photograph school and club cheer squads across the Houston area, from game-day routines on the sideline to team portraits. I stay near the squad rather than the ball, so every cheerleader is in more frames, not just the ones at the end of the game.

The photos on this page are the Trinity Lutheran School cheer team at the football game against The Woodlands Christian Academy, shot at 5800 Academy Way, The Woodlands, TX 77384. See the football gallery from the same game.
