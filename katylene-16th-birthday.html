Katylene’s Sweet 16

Project Type
Sweet 16 Portraits
When
2026
Location
Houston, TX

Sixteen — the first birthday a girl wants photographed properly.

A sweet sixteen session sits in an unusual place. She is old enough to have opinions about how she looks and young enough that her mother is still the one booking it, and the photographs have to satisfy both.

So the job is to photograph her the way she sees herself now, not the way she was photographed as a child. Less arranging, more letting her find her own posture — and the frames her mum will want come out of that anyway.
