Senior Portraits

Everett’s Senior Portraits

Project Type
Senior Portraits & Family
When
October 2026
Location
Welch Park, Jacobs Lake Blvd, Conroe, TX

A drummer’s senior session: the kit first, then the cap and gown.

Everett is a high school senior who loves music, and the drums are the part of him his family wanted in the pictures. So we started at home, in the music room where his kit is set up. I didn’t pose him. He sat down and played, the way he does when nobody is watching, and I photographed that.

Then we headed to Welch Park, on Jacobs Lake Blvd, a few minutes from the house, for the classic senior portraits in his cap and gown. He brought his drumsticks along, so the outdoor frames have a bit of the music room in them too.

His family joined in for a few relaxed family moments, with plenty of laughing, and we finished with candids of Everett on his own. A senior session is for the one person who is about to leave school behind. The best frames are the ones that look like him.
