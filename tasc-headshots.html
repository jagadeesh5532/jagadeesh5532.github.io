TASC Coaching Staff Headshots

Project Type
Staff Headshots
When
2026
Location
Tomball Area Swim and Dive Club

A whole coaching staff, photographed between sessions.

The Tomball Area Swim and Dive Club needed headshots of its coaches for the club website. That is a different job from photographing one person who has booked an hour for themselves — coaches are working, swimmers are in the water, and nobody has set aside the afternoon to be photographed.

So the shoot came to them. We worked through the staff one at a time, in daylight, with the pool and deck behind rather than a backdrop, and kept each person in front of the camera for only a few minutes. Most of them were in coaching kit, which is right: these portraits sit on a swim club’s site, not a law firm’s.

The patience matters more than the lighting here. Half of a coaching staff will tell you they hate having their photo taken, and the job is to get each of them to a frame they are happy to have on a public page.

Why a swim club came to me for headshots

This one came out of the swimming. I have been the official photographer for TASC and Northpointe Shockwaves for several summers — meets, divisionals, awards nights, thousands of action frames of kids in the water. When the Tomball Area Swim and Dive Club needed portraits of its coaching staff for the website, they already knew how I work and did not have to audition anybody.

That matters more than it sounds. A club with two locations and a seasonal staff does not have a spare afternoon for a studio booking, and coaches are not models. Knowing the deck, the light at that time of day and most of the people in front of the lens meant the whole staff was done in one visit, between sessions, without the swimming schedule bending around me.

If you run a club, a studio, a practice or a small team and need everyone photographed consistently, this is the easiest version of a headshot job: I come to you, work through your people one at a time, and you get a matching set that sits properly together on one page. No studio hire, no asking staff to travel on their own time.

You can read how I approach headshots, see the swim team work this grew out of, or get in touch about your staff.
