A Houston Courthouse Wedding

Project Type
Courthouse Wedding
When
August 2026
Location
Downtown Houston, TX

One hour, one room, one family — a courthouse wedding downtown, and everything that matters in it.

Charlene and Joshua were married at the Houston Municipal Courthouse on a weekday afternoon. No venue, no reception, no months of planning — a room on the second floor, the people who needed to be there, and about an hour start to finish.

Courthouse weddings ask something different of a photographer. No rehearsal, no second run at anything. The ceremony is short, the room is small and often bright in awkward ways, and you work around officials doing their job rather than a coordinator managing yours. Everything has to be right the first time.

What they have in abundance is focus. Nothing sits between a couple and the reason they came — and the last frame of the day was the three of them, Charlene, Joshua and their daughter, on the day it became official.
