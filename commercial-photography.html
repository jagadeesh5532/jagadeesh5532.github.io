Commercial Photographer, Houston & Katy

Stores, restaurants, offices and brands — clean, consistent photographs for your website, listings and launch.

Photographs your business can use for years

Commercial photography is different from event work. Nothing is happening around you; the job is to make a space, a product or a team look the way you want customers to see it, and to keep that look consistent across your website, your listings, your social media and your print.

I photograph retail stores and showrooms, restaurants and venues, offices and workspaces, and the people who work in them. I plan the shot list with you first, so the displays, entrances, layouts and details that matter to your customers are covered, then shoot with controlled light and clean angles.

Recent work includes the new Better Baseball store at Park Row in Katy, Texas, the Georgia retailer’s first Texas location. For events, launches and conferences, see corporate and VIP event photography.

If you need individual portraits of the team as well, see headshots.

Stores, restaurants, offices & brand photography

Retail and store photography. Interiors, displays, product walls, entrances and storefronts, for an opening, a website or a listing. I photograph the space as customers will find it, then edit the set so the colour and light are consistent.

Restaurants and venues. The room, the counter, the table and the detail, for menus, websites and booking pages.

Offices and teams. The workspace and the people in it, such as the MayaTax office photoshoot in Houston, delivered in color and black and white for the firm’s website and marketing.

Where I work. Stores and offices across Houston, Katy and the Grand Parkway corridor, The Woodlands, Sugar Land, Cypress and Tomball. If you are opening a new location, I can photograph it as soon as it is ready, before or after opening day.

What you get

  • A shot list planned with you before the day
  • Interiors, displays, storefronts and product detail
  • Consistent colour and light across the whole set
  • Team and staff portraits at your location
  • Web-ready and print-resolution files, sized for your website, listings and social
  • Delivery in a few days, faster when you have a launch date
  • Photographs for new locations, openings and rebrands

Questions people ask

What is commercial photography?

Photographs made for a business to use in its own marketing: store and interior photography, product and display images, restaurant and venue photography, and team or brand portraits for your website, listings, social media and print.

Do you photograph stores, restaurants and offices?

Yes. I photograph retail stores, restaurants, showrooms and offices across Houston, Katy, The Woodlands and the surrounding suburbs, including the new Better Baseball store at Park Row in Katy.

How do you shoot a store or interior?

I plan the shot list with you first: the displays, the layout, the entrance and the details you want customers to see. I shoot with consistent light and clean angles, then edit for colour and consistency so the set looks like one brand.

How fast do you deliver commercial photos?

Most commercial sets are delivered within a few days, and a quick selection can often be turned around sooner if you have a launch or opening date.

Who owns the images?

You get full use of the delivered images for your own marketing, press, website and listings. I retain copyright and the right to show the work in my own portfolio; tell me if anything should stay private.
