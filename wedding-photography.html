Wedding Photography

Full days, short ceremonies and everything between, photographed across Houston and the towns around it.

A day you only get one pass at

A wedding does not wait for the photographer. The morning drags and then the whole thing is over, and the parts people remember afterwards are rarely the ones anybody scheduled — a mother straightening a veil, a father looking away, the breath someone takes before the doors open.

I photograph weddings across Houston, Katy, Missouri City, Pearland and the rest of the area. Full-day coverage with getting-ready through to the last dance, or a courthouse ceremony downtown that takes twenty minutes. Both get the same attention; they just need different plans.

A good many of the couples I work with are marrying across two cultures, with two sets of rituals in one day — a Nikkah and a reception, a temple ceremony and a Western one. Those days are busier and, for a photographer, far more interesting. Tell me how yours runs and I will build the timeline around it rather than the other way round.

What you get

  • Coverage built around your timeline, not a fixed package clock
  • A second shooter available when the day needs one
  • An online gallery with high-resolution files and web-sized copies
  • No cap on the number of edited photographs
  • Normal turnaround is five to seven days
  • Prints and albums available on request after delivery

Questions people ask

How far in advance should we book our wedding?

Anywhere from a few months out to a few weeks. I have taken bookings the day before and I have held dates three or four months ahead — it depends entirely on what else is on the calendar. If you have a date, ask early; it costs nothing to check.

Do you need a deposit to hold the date?

Yes. A deposit secures the date, and the amount depends on the scope of the day. The balance is settled around delivery.

Is a second photographer available?

Yes. For a full day with getting-ready in two places, a large guest count, or a ceremony and reception at separate venues, a second shooter is worth it. For a courthouse ceremony or a small gathering it usually is not.

How long until we see our photos?

Normally five to seven days for the full gallery. If you need a handful of images sooner — for a thank-you post or to send to family — say so and I will pull a preview set out early.

Do you travel outside Houston?

Yes. Houston and the surrounding suburbs are routine — Katy, Pearland, Sugar Land, Tomball, Spring, Galveston and everything between. I also take travel assignments further afield when a couple wants me there, with travel costs added.
