Where I Shoot

Based in Cypress, working across Houston and the surrounding suburbs — and further afield when a job calls for it.

From the bay to the Brazos

I am based in Cypress, northwest of Houston, and most of my work happens across the city and the suburbs around it. That covers a lot of ground — the coast at Galveston in one direction, College Station in another, and everything from courthouse steps downtown to a back garden in Tomball in between.

The towns below are places I have actually photographed, with the galleries to show for it. If yours is not listed, it almost certainly still works; ask and I will tell you straight whether the drive makes sense.

Beyond that radius I still travel, with a travel cost added. Several couples have brought me further than I would have guessed, and I have yet to regret a long drive.
