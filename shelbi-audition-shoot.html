Shelbi — Audition Headshots

Project Type
Audition & Model Headshots
When
2026
Location
Terry Hershey Park, Houston, TX

An aspiring model needed shots she could actually submit.

Shelbi is early in her modelling career and needed a set for auditions and casting submissions. That is a particular brief. Casting directors are not looking for a flattering photograph — they are looking for an accurate one, with the face clearly readable and nothing in the frame competing for attention.

They also want range. One pose repeated twelve times tells them nothing, so we worked through variations across the session: close headshots, three-quarter frames, full length, expression changed between each.

We shot outdoors in natural light rather than in a studio. For someone building a first portfolio that is the sensible choice — it is quicker, it costs less, and daylight on a clean background holds up perfectly well against studio lighting for submission work.

Why Terry Hershey Park suits a first portfolio

We shot at Terry Hershey Park, which runs about six miles along Buffalo Bayou in west Houston — 496 acres and eleven miles of trail, named for the conservationist who fought in the 1960s to stop the bayou banks being concreted over. It opens at seven in the morning and costs nothing.

For audition work the useful thing is how plain it is. Casting submissions want the face doing the work, so you need backgrounds that recede rather than compete: unbroken green, open trail, a shaded stretch under trees that behaves like a softbox. Within a short walk the park gives you several of those, which is how a set gets variety without looking like it was taken in four different cities.

The light is the other half. Deep shade under the tree cover gives even, directionless light with no hard shadow across the face, and the open trail gives something brighter when you want contrast. That combination is why an outdoor session can stand in for a studio at this stage — and it is considerably less intimidating for someone being photographed properly for the first time.

If you need audition, casting or portfolio shots: bring more changes than you think you need, keep the clothing plain and solid-coloured, and book for the first or last two hours of daylight. You can read what to wear, see how I photograph headshots, or get in touch.
