Natalia’s 1st Birthday

Project Type
First Birthday Celebration
When
2026
Location
Sugar Land Country Club, TX

One year old, and already faster than the photographer.

A first birthday at Sugar Land Country Club, with more friends and family in the room than Natalia had any idea what to do with.

Children this age do not pose and do not stay put, so the work is mostly movement — following her around the room, staying low, and waiting for the half-second where she stops and looks up. The family portraits happened early, while everyone was still tidy and nobody had met the cake.

A first birthday is really the parents’ milestone as much as the child’s. She will not remember it. They will, and so will everyone who flew in for it.
