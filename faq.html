Common Questions

The things people ask me before they book — answered plainly.

Ask me anything that isn’t here

These are the questions that come up most often: turnaround time, how many photographs you get, what to wear, whether the dog can come. If yours is not below, send it over — there is no such thing as a question that is too small to ask before you book somebody to photograph a day you cannot repeat.

Questions people ask

How long until I get my photos?

Normally five to seven days for a full gallery. Corporate work and headshots are faster — a couple of days, sometimes under twenty-four hours. If you have a deadline, tell me when you book and I will work to it.

How many photos will I get?

I don’t put a cap on it. You get everything worth keeping, which for a one-hour session is usually at least thirty to forty edited images and often a good deal more. It varies with the type of session: a full wedding day produces far more than a headshot sitting.

How are the photos delivered?

Through an online gallery, with high-resolution files for printing and web-sized copies for sharing. You download them yourself, and you can share the gallery link with family.

Can I order prints or a photo book?

Yes, on request once your gallery is delivered. Prints and books are priced separately — tell me what you have in mind and we will go through the options.

How far ahead should I book?

Anywhere from the next day to three or four months out. Some of my best sessions were booked with a day’s notice. If you have a fixed date, particularly a wedding, ask early.

Do you take a deposit?

Yes, to hold the date. The amount depends on the scope of the job, and the balance is settled around delivery.

Where do you travel?

Houston and the surrounding suburbs as standard — Katy, Pearland, Sugar Land, Tomball, Spring, Galveston, Conroe and everything between. I also take travel assignments beyond that, with travel costs added.

What happens if it rains?

We reschedule to a date that suits us both. There is no sense fighting Texas weather, and no outdoor session is worth doing in bad light and wet ground.

How long is a session?

Portrait and maternity sessions run between forty-five minutes and an hour and a half. Events and weddings are built around your timeline instead.

I’m awkward in front of a camera. Will you help me pose?

Yes. Almost everybody says this, and almost nobody means it by the end. I will place you, adjust as we go, and if you have seen photographs you like, send them — working from something concrete is far easier than guessing.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. Pets are welcome, and they tend to relax everybody. Mention it when you book so I can pick a location that suits.

What should I wear?

Tell me where we are shooting and who is coming, and I will give you specific suggestions. In general: solid colours over busy patterns, coordinate rather than match, and bring a second option if you have one.

Can you suggest a location?

Yes, and I would rather you asked than picked somewhere blind. Tell me the group size, the time you can get there and the look you want, and I will suggest places around Houston that work at that hour.

Is a second photographer available for weddings?

Yes. For a long day, a large guest count, or a ceremony and reception in different places, a second shooter is worth it. For a small ceremony it usually isn’t, and I will say so.

Do you photograph cultural and religious ceremonies?

Yes — baptisms, christenings, Nikkah ceremonies, Diwali and more. Tell me how your ceremony runs and which moments matter most to your family, and I will plan around that rather than guessing.

Do you work with non-profit organisations?

Yes. Fundraisers, volunteer days, community programmes and donor events.
