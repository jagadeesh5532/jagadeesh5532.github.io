# Google Ads: Senior Portraits Test Campaign

Budget: $10/day (~$300/month). Landing page: https://jg-moments.com/senior-portrait-photography.html

I can't create the Google Ads account or enter billing details for you, so follow the steps below (about 15 minutes).

## Before you start
1. Go to ads.google.com and sign in with the Google account that owns your Business Profile.
2. If it pushes "Smart campaign" or "Performance Max", click **Switch to Expert Mode** (bottom of the page), then **Create a campaign without a goal**. This gives full control and avoids wasted spend.
3. Add billing last, when it asks.

## Campaign settings
- Campaign type: **Search**
- Name: `Senior Portraits - Houston`
- Goal: Leads (or no goal)
- Results: Website visits plus Phone calls, if you want calls
- Networks: **Uncheck Search Partners and Display Network** (search only)
- Bidding: **Maximize clicks**, with a max CPC cap of **$2.50**. After about 15 conversions, switch to Maximize conversions.
- Daily budget: **$10**
- Locations: Target Houston, The Woodlands, Conroe, Spring, Tomball, Cypress, Katy, Cinco Ranch, Sugar Land (add as radius 20 miles around Tomball/Cypress if easier). Choose **Presence** (people in the location), not "interest".
- Language: English
- Ad schedule: all days (adjust later using data)
- Audience: Observation only, no targeting. Skip.

## Conversion tracking (important)
Without it you can't tell what works.
1. In Ads: Goals > Conversions > New conversion action > Website.
2. Track the contact form submission (`contact.html` thank-you state) or a click on the phone/email link.
3. Quick option: link Google Analytics (GA4) and import key events. Tell me and I'll add the tag or event code to the site.

## What to check
- Week 1: Search terms report. Add junk queries as negatives.
- Week 2: Pause keywords with 20+ clicks and no inquiries.
- Day 30: Judge cost per inquiry. A good target is under $40 per lead on a $300 session.
- Expect roughly 100-200 clicks/month at this budget. Seniors book in a short window (summer and fall), so run it during peak season.

## Don't
- Don't accept "recommendations" that raise your budget.
- Don't enable auto-applied assets or broad match.
