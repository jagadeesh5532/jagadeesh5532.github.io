Swim Meet Photography

Competitive swimming photographed at speed, from starts to finishes.

Starts, turns, and the touch

A swim meet is long and almost entirely waiting, punctuated by thirty seconds that matter. The photographs worth having are narrow: the start off the blocks, the turn, the stretch into the wall, and the look up at the board afterwards.

I photograph meets around Houston and the surrounding suburbs for swim clubs, school teams and individual families. Pool decks are crowded and busy, so I shoot from fixed positions and work with the heat sheet rather than wandering.

What parents get back is their own swimmer — not a lane full of churn, but the one race they came to watch, including the part at the end where they find you in the stands.
