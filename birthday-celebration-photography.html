Birthdays & Celebrations

First birthdays, Sweet 16s, milestone years, graduations and the parties families spend months arranging.

Everyone in one room, finally

Birthdays, graduations, Christmas, a seventy-fifth with four generations in the same house — these are days families spend months arranging and then barely get to see, because somebody is always in the kitchen.

I photograph celebrations across Houston, Spring, Galveston and the surrounding towns. Sometimes it is a hall with a hundred guests; sometimes a living room with fifteen people and a cake. Either way the approach is the same: stay out of the way, keep moving, and come back with the room as it actually was.

The photographs people pick out afterwards are rarely the posed group shot. They are the grandmother laughing at something off-camera, the cousins crowded onto a sofa, the child who has given up and fallen asleep on somebody’s shoulder. I will still get the group shot — but that is not the one that ends up framed.

What you get

  • Candid coverage of the whole event, not just the cake
  • Family and group portraits while everyone is still in one place
  • Venue, home, park or hall — wherever it is happening
  • An online gallery with high-resolution files and web-sized copies
  • No cap on the number of edited photographs
  • Normal turnaround is five to seven days

Questions people ask

How long do you stay at a party?

As long as the part you want covered lasts. Most parties need two to three hours — arrivals, the cake or the speeches, and enough afterwards to catch people relaxed. Tell me the run of the evening and we will work out the window.

Can you photograph at our house rather than a venue?

Yes, and plenty of the best ones are at home. A living room full of family beats a hired hall for atmosphere every time.

Will you take group photos as well as candids?

Yes. I will round people up for family and group shots while everyone is still in one place, usually before the food, then go back to working the room.

Do you photograph children’s parties?

Often. Children do not hold still and that is the point — I work around them rather than trying to pose them, and let the fun happen.

How soon do we get the gallery?

Five to seven days normally.
