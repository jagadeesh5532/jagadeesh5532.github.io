Cultural & Religious Ceremonies

Baptisms, christenings, Nikkah ceremonies, Diwali and the traditions a family has kept for generations.

Traditions worth keeping properly

These are the days a family has been part of for generations, and the photographs end up outliving everyone in them. They deserve to be done properly.

I have photographed baptisms and christenings in Pearland and Sugar Land, Nikkah ceremonies in Katy, and Indian celebrations across Houston. Every tradition has its own order of events, its own moments that matter and its own that do not, and I would far rather ask beforehand than guess on the day.

So tell me how yours runs. Knowing when the important moment is coming — the aarti, the anointing, the signing, the first lamp — is most of the job. The rest is staying out of the way of the people whose day it is, and being in the right place when it arrives.

What you get

  • A conversation beforehand about the order of the ceremony and what matters most
  • Discreet coverage inside the service, working within whatever the venue allows
  • Family and group portraits afterwards while everyone is together
  • An online gallery with high-resolution files and web-sized copies
  • No cap on the number of edited photographs
  • Normal turnaround is five to seven days

Questions people ask

We have a tradition you may not have photographed before. Is that a problem?

Not at all — it is the reason I ask. Walk me through how the day runs and which moments your family considers the important ones, and I will plan around that rather than guessing.

Can you photograph inside the church or mosque?

Usually, within whatever the venue permits. Some restrict movement during the service, some ask for no flash. Tell me which venue and I will check the rules beforehand so nothing is a surprise on the day.

Will you take family portraits afterwards?

Yes, and it is worth planning for. Everyone is dressed, everyone is in one place, and that will not happen again for a long time. Fifteen minutes is usually enough.

Do you cover Diwali and other community events?

Yes — temple celebrations, community gatherings, office events and family evenings at home, across the Houston area.

How soon do we get the photographs?

Five to seven days normally.
