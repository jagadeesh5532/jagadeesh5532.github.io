Wedding Story

Dominque & Joshua

Project Type
Post-Wedding Portraits
When
June 2026
Location
Houston, TX

A wedding day that went by too fast — and the second chance that put it right.

Dominque’s wedding photos came back missing something: not one frame of her alone in her gown, and barely any of just the two of them. It is among the most common regrets there is — the day moves so fast the couple ends up in the background of their own wedding.

So we set up at her home a few weeks later: backdrop, lights, a cleared corner of the living room, and the portraits the wedding day never made room for. Her on her own. The two of them, unhurried. And a set with her mother, which is the kind of picture families keep for a very long time.

Working at home has an advantage no venue can match — no schedule pressing, no guests waiting, no coordinator counting down.

★★★★★

“After receiving our initial wedding photos back, we realized I didn’t get 1 photo by myself in my wedding gown nor too many of us by ourselves.. so we scheduled a photoshoot! The photographer came to our home & was amazing! This was so fun & intimate! It was wild having to get back into these wedding clothes 3 weeks later because so many emotions from that day came back, but it was so worth it! Thank you to @jg_moments for making this happen for us! We appreciate you!”

Dominque Joshua — Wedding Photography
