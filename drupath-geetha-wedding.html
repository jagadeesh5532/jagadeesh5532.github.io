Wedding Story

Drupath & Geetha

Project Type
Courthouse Wedding
When
June 2026
Location
Houston, TX

A morning ceremony, then an afternoon in the park — a small Houston wedding that made room for both.

Drupath and Geetha married in Houston one morning with a couple of friends alongside them. Small, civil, and done well before lunch.

What made it work was what came after. Rather than ending at the door, we drove out to Terry Hershey Park for couple portraits under the trees — the two of them out of the formality of the ceremony room, in ordinary daylight with nothing scheduled.

It is a pairing I recommend often for small weddings. The ceremony gives you the record of the day; the session afterwards gives you the photographs you hang on a wall. One is brisk and procedural, the other unhurried, and a single morning covers the whole story.

“Photos were delivered very quickly and Jag was professional and captured all moments perfectly. We are happy with the experience.”

Drupath Reddy — Engagement Photography

A courthouse morning, then Terry Hershey Park

Drupath and Geetha married in front of a judge at the Harris County Clerk’s Chimney Rock Annex on Chimney Rock Road, with a couple of friends standing with them. Civil ceremonies there are booked by appointment and run to the clock — you are in, married, and back out in a matter of minutes. Lovely, but brisk. There is no aisle and no long exit, so if the photographs matter to you, the ceremony alone will not give you them.

That is why we drove west afterwards. Terry Hershey Park runs for about six miles along Buffalo Bayou in west Houston — 496 acres and eleven miles of trail, named for the conservationist who fought in the 1960s to stop the bayou banks being paved over. Twenty minutes from the annex, open from seven in the morning, and free.

What makes it work for a post-ceremony session is how quickly the trail changes character. Open grass, then dense tree cover, then the bayou itself, all within a short walk — so you get genuine variety without anybody having to get back in a car in wedding clothes. And on a weekday morning it is quiet. These two were easy together, visibly relieved the formal part was done, and that shows in every frame.

If you are planning a small civil wedding in Houston, I would pair it with a park session the same morning every time. The ceremony is the record; the hour afterwards is where the photographs come from. You can see how I cover weddings, read a little about me, or get in touch about your date.
